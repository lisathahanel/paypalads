At PayPalAds.com, we respect your privacy and want you to understand how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PayPalAds.com. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and handled when you visit, browse, submit an advertisement, contact us, or otherwise use our website.

PayPalAds.com provides classified advertising services together with learning, informational and entertainment content.

By using the website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please discontinue use of the website.

2. Information We May Collect

The information collected may depend on how you use PayPalAds.com. Some visitors may simply browse the website, while others may submit advertisements or contact us.

Information You Provide

When you submit an advertisement, contact form or other information, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone or other contact details
  • Advertisement title and description
  • Product, property, job or service information
  • Images or other content you choose to upload
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

You should avoid including unnecessary sensitive or confidential information in a public advertisement.

3. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit a website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the website, hosting providers, analytics services, advertising services or other technologies.

This may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location based on technical information
  • Date and time of visits
  • Referring pages
  • General interaction with the website

This information may be used to understand website usage, improve performance, maintain security and provide relevant website features.

4. Classified Advertisements

When you publish an advertisement, information included in that advertisement may become publicly visible.

For example, an advertisement may contain a name, telephone number, email address, location, product details, property information or other information that you intentionally include in the listing.

Privacy Tip: Before publishing an advertisement, carefully check the information you are sharing. Do not publish passwords, financial account details, identity documents or other highly sensitive personal information.

5. How We May Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating PayPalAds.com.
  • Publishing and managing advertisements.
  • Responding to questions and requests.
  • Communicating with users about submitted content.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.
  • Monitoring website performance.
  • Detecting spam, abuse, fraud or security problems.
  • Maintaining and protecting the website.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PayPalAds.com may use cookies and similar technologies.

A cookie is a small piece of information stored by a website in your browser. Cookies can help websites remember settings, understand traffic and provide certain functionality.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Website functionality
  • Security
  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • Remembering preferences

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

7. Advertising

PayPalAds.com may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners.

These advertising providers may use cookies, device information, advertising identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable requirements.

Advertising providers may process information independently from PayPalAds.com. Their collection and use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Google Advertising Services

PayPalAds.com may use Google advertising services, including Google AdSense or related technologies.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements. Depending on the user's location, advertising personalization and consent requirements may apply.

Visitors can learn more about Google's advertising and privacy practices through Google's own privacy and advertising information.

9. Third-Party Services

PayPalAds.com may use third-party providers to support website functions. These may include services for forms, analytics, advertising, hosting, security, content delivery, email or other technical functions.

These providers may process certain information as necessary to provide their services.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review the privacy information provided by those services when appropriate.

10. Contact Forms

If you contact PayPalAds.com through a website form, the information you submit may be processed by the service used to operate that form.

This may include your name, email address, message and any other information you voluntarily provide.

We use such information to respond to your communication and handle legitimate website-related requests.

11. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information simply because you use PayPalAds.com.

Information may, however, be shared with service providers or other parties when reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide a requested service, protect the website and its users, or comply with applicable law.

Public information that you intentionally place in a classified advertisement may naturally be visible to other website visitors.

12. Legal and Security Reasons

Information may be retained, reviewed or disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or a valid legal request.
  • Investigate suspected fraud or abuse.
  • Protect the security of the website.
  • Protect users or other people from harm.
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions.
  • Protect our legal rights or property.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website.

However, no website, online service or method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should therefore take appropriate care when sharing personal information online.

14. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to operate the website, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, meet legal obligations or protect legitimate interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is being retained.

15. Children's Privacy

PayPalAds.com is not intended to encourage children to provide personal information unnecessarily.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

16. External Websites

PayPalAds.com may contain links to other websites.

Once you leave PayPalAds.com and visit another website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

17. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or information about the processing of certain personal information, subject to applicable legal limitations.

You may also be able to control cookies and certain advertising preferences through your browser, device settings or available third-party privacy controls.

18. Removing or Updating an Advertisement

If you have published an advertisement and need to correct or remove information, use the available tools provided by PayPalAds.com or contact us through the website.

We may need sufficient information to identify the relevant advertisement before making changes or processing a request.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the website, services, technologies or applicable legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

20. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, personal information or an advertisement containing your information, please contact PayPalAds.com through the contact method provided on the website.

Your Privacy Matters

PayPalAds.com is built to provide a simple place to Buy, Sell, Find and Promote, while also providing learning, information and entertainment.

We encourage every visitor and advertiser to think carefully about the information they choose to share online.
