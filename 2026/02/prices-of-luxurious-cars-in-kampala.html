The 2026 car market in Kampala is offering surprisingly good deals on luxury vehicles. Several high-end SUVs and sedans have dropped significantly in price, making them more accessible to buyers.

🚘 Mercedes GLE 2017 Model

A luxurious SUV known for its commanding road presence and elegant design. The interior is spacious and comfortable, offering both diesel and petrol variants.

  • Fuel Economy (Diesel): 6–7 liters per 100 km
  • Fuel Economy (Petrol): 7–12 liters per 100 km
  • Old Price: UGX 240 million
  • Current Price (2026): UGX 165–170 million

The diesel version is recommended for better fuel efficiency and long-term savings.

🚘 BMW X6 2018 Model

A high-end midsize SUV with a sporty coupe-like profile. The 2018 X6 features a premium red interior and a futuristic infotainment system.

  • Fuel Economy (Petrol): 9–12 liters per 100 km
  • Fuel Economy (Diesel): 6–8 liters per 100 km
  • Price: UGX 165 million

Known for its powerful performance and sporty driving experience.

🚘 Mercedes C200 2018 Model

An entry-level luxury sedan offering class and comfort without being overly flashy. It features a futuristic dashboard, pure leather interior, and multiple safety airbags.

  • Fuel Economy (Diesel): 4–5 liters per 100 km
  • Fuel Economy (Petrol): 6–7 liters per 100 km
  • Price: UGX 73 million

A great option for buyers looking for luxury at a more affordable price point.

🚘 Jeep Wrangler 2017 Model

Built for adventure and off-road performance, this 3.6L V6-powered SUV is designed for durability and capability.

  • Fuel Consumption: 7–8 km per liter
  • Engine: 3.6L V6
  • Unique Feature: Removable roof for open-air driving
  • Price: UGX 150 million

Ideal for safari drives and rugged terrain rather than fuel economy.

🚘 Mazda CX-5 2017 Model

A mid-range SUV growing in popularity in Kampala. It is considered a more affordable alternative to the Toyota Harrier.

  • Strong diesel torque performance
  • Modern leather-coated interior
  • Price: Approximately UGX 70 million

A practical choice for buyers who want style, performance, and affordability.

📉 Why Cars Are Cheaper in 2026

The current market conditions in Kampala have made luxury vehicles more affordable compared to previous years. Buyers can now access high-end SUVs and sedans at significantly reduced prices.
